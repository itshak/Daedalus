# Code of Conduct

Daedalus welcomes constructive participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Our shared goal is to make a safe, maintainable open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and precise when discussing technical tradeoffs.
- Assume good faith, while remaining open to corrections and evidence.
- Give actionable feedback about code and ideas rather than personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal information, credentials, or private game/account data.
- Credit upstream maintainers and contributors accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing, personal insults, and repeated disruptive conduct are not welcome. Deliberately publishing private credentials or encouraging others to do so is also unacceptable.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove inappropriate content; close or lock threads; and restrict participation when needed. Action depends on the severity and recurrence of the conduct. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related community spaces.

For a public platform-abuse report, use GitHub's built-in **Report content** controls. To raise a concern privately with the project owner, use the contact methods on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/itshak). Do not expose sensitive personal details in a public issue.

This policy can evolve as the community grows.
